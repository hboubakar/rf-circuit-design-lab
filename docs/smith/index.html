      How the moves work

      • Series keeps r fixed: the point slides on its resistance circle. Clockwise adds an inductor, anticlockwise a capacitor.
      • Shunt keeps g fixed: the point slides on its conductance circle. Clockwise adds a capacitor, anticlockwise an inductor.
      • Line turns the point clockwise around the centre at constant |Γ|. Half a wavelength is one full turn.
      • Stub is a shunt element made of line: it slides on the conductance circle too. Choose open or short and its Zc under the chart.
